IT Career Compass

Offline IT Career Aptitude and Potential Assessment

Explore your reasoning, learning potential, interests and possible IT career pathways.

Candidate details

Suggested time: 35–50 minutes. No previous IT experience is required.

Important: This is an exploratory prototype, not a validated psychometric test. It cannot guarantee success or rule out a career in IT.

Part A: Objective reasoning

10 questions. One point per question. Select one answer unless instructed otherwise.

Part B: Learning potential

Learn this rule

If a number is greater than 5, display PASS. Otherwise, display REVIEW.
Examples: 8 → PASS; 3 → REVIEW; 5 → REVIEW.

Stage 1: Initial understanding

Stage 2: Transfer to a new situation

A delivery weighing 10 kg or less goes in a VAN. A delivery weighing more than 10 kg goes in a TRUCK.

Stage 3: After reviewing feedback

A support ticket waiting more than 4 hours is flagged LATE. Otherwise, it is flagged ON TIME.

For a fair experiment, review the rule and any errors after the first two stages, then complete Stage 3.

Part C: Career interests

Rate each activity from 1 (strongly dislike) to 5 (strongly like).

Part D: Work approach

Rate how often each statement describes you. 1 = almost never; 5 = almost always.
